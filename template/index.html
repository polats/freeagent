This is the freeagent template

Nothing runs here. This Space exists so the landing page can duplicate it into a Space of your own, with a fresh COLLIE_AUTH_TOKEN, where Herdr, Collie and your coding agents actually run.

To do it by hand: use Duplicate this Space in the menu above, set the secret COLLIE_AUTH_TOKEN to 24+ random characters, remove the FREEAGENT_TEMPLATE variable, and open your copy at /#token=<that token>.
